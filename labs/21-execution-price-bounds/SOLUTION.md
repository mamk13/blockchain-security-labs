# Solution and design trade-off

Keep the user-selected minimum immutable across the attempted trade. Stage output and check both predicates before mutating ledgers. Deadline equality is accepted; one time unit later rejects. The suite also enumerates847 small reserve/input combinations for input/output conservation and nondecreasing product. This is not a proof of a deployed AMM.

A passing model is a reproducible boundary argument, not production certification. Keep the negative control: it proves the suite can distinguish the intended failure.
